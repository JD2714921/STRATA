## Guardrails

- Each reference retains its supplied timeframe. The new Nikkei 225 and US 20Y yield references are shown at 1D; they are not relabelled as weekly. There is no lower-timeframe switch on this surface.
- Bond-price series and yield series remain distinct. STRATA must never invert, substitute or compare them as though they were the same measure.
- The screenshot capture time is not supplied. Current values, candle coordinates, liquidity levels, direction, bias and signals are not extracted from pixels.
- The later calculation path must join a timestamped event expectation to verified OHLC data at the reference timeframe, mark the pre-event liquidity reference, measure the post-event response over a declared window, and retain provider provenance.
- Commodity provider and contract mappings are not visible on the supplied images, so no proxy contract is silently substituted. Sugar White is context-only; the traded sugar instrument remains Sugar Raw Cash.
- The supplied FX screenshots retain the currency-pair direction shown. AUDUSD is a separate reference from the existing USDAUD watchlist item; no inverse-series substitution is made.
- The generic Strategy reference is replaced with MSTR / MicroStrategy. COIN and MSTR are equity references; no standalone crypto-asset ticker is added as a substitute.
