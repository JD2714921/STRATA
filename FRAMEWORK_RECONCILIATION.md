`reconcileFrameworks` reports agreement, contradiction, independent-interpretation, missing-evidence or non-applicability. A comparison is displayed as context only if both sides have scoped, timestamp-eligible observations and matching horizons/topics. It does not conclude that either theory has been demonstrated. Contradictory evidence within a concept remains attached alongside supporting evidence; there is no vote, blended score or forced synthesis.

The PDF’s p.12 sovereign sell-off, undervalued stock and oil-spike scenarios are illustrative educational examples. They have no observed prices, sample counts or validated outcomes and are not historical tests. Present implementation fixtures are explicitly synthetic. A missing or unmatched evidence set can return **NO RELEVANT FRAMEWORK APPLIES** or **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**.

Before an annotation’s ingestion time, the engine returns no concept text or comparison annotations to historical consumers. The educational library browser always labels itself as the current library; it is not part of replay inputs.
